How it works

  1. Add a passage by reference. Each verse appears in Greek with a translation box under it.
  2. Write your translation in the box. It saves as you type.
  3. Click any word for its dictionary entry and panel below the verse. Identify its parsing (person, tense, voice, mood, number, or case, number, gender), then Check. Wrong fields show the right answer.
  4. Verbs also offer Conjugate: pick a tense, voice and mood and fill in the six forms. Nouns, adjectives, pronouns and articles offer Decline. Forms attested in the New Testament are marked sblgnt; regular paradigms the app generates are marked generated. Where neither exists, the cell is left for you to check against your grammar.
  5. Done collapses the panel and underlines the word: green when you had it right, red when you were corrected.
  6. Export your Greek, translation and parsing notes as Markdown, plain text or a printed page. A JSON backup restores the whole session.
  7. Click a word you do not know and its meaning stays under it, so the verse fills in as you work through it. Hide meanings in the passage header clears them again, and an option shows every word's meaning at once. Under each passage a Glossary lists every distinct word, rarest first.
  8. Install it from your browser menu (or the Install app button) to get an icon on your phone or desktop. Passages you have opened stay readable offline.
