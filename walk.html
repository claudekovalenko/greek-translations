Endings cheat sheet

Spotting the verb

  • ἐ‑ at the front (the augment, or a lengthened first vowel): past time, indicative only. Imperfect, aorist or pluperfect.
  • σ before the ending: future (λύσω) or first aorist (ἔλυσα).
  • θη / θε: aorist passive (ἐλύθην); θησ: future passive.
  • Reduplication (λελυκα) and κα: perfect.
  • Long vowel ω / η in the ending (λύωμεν, λύητε): subjunctive.
